1. Property remains the largest exposure, but is deleveraging

30.3% of total loans (HK$3.27tn), down 2.5 ppt YoY. Developer loans fell 9.1% while residential mortgages still grew 3.7%, so the property book is shifting from developers to households.

2. Trade-related lending is the growth engine

Trade financing +15.9% YoY to HK$436bn, the fastest of all focus segments; manufacturing +11.9%, wholesale & retail +6.3%. Combined share up to 10.3% of total loans, about one-third the size of property lending.

3. Trade finance supply is concentrated

Top 10 lenders hold 76% of the market and the top 3 (HSBC, Standard Chartered, BOC) about 53%. HSBC and Hang Seng together account for about 37%, so supply depends heavily on a few large banking groups.
